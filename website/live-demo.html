04 / THE COMPLETE SYSTEM

Why this control loop matters.

A visual tour of features already present in PortalPulse and what this hosted prototype demonstrates.

⌁

1. Observe

Measured rate, concurrency, latency, failures, queue depth, and DB pressure from local portal requests.

↗

2. Forecast

Loaded XGBoost model estimates 5, 10, and 15 minute demand and capacity pressure.

◎

3. Detect

Isolation Forest scores unusual behavior and names contributing metrics.

◇

4. Diagnose

Root-cause classifier and NetworkX path connect symptoms to infrastructure.

✧

5. Decide

Loaded PyTorch MLP ranks actions. Capability masking and M6 safety rules check execution.

↻

6. Verify

M7 applies an approved action locally. Measured telemetry determines verified recovery or failure.

WHERE IT FITS

How it complements edge protection.

Cloudflare style edge controls can filter, cache, and rate limit traffic before it reaches a portal. PortalPulse's hosted prototype focuses on internal portal behavior: model based forecast and diagnosis, safety checked action, and measured post-action verification. These are complementary layers, not a benchmark claim.
